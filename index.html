How I work

01

I start at the bottleneck, not the ticket.

Before writing anything I want to know what's actually slow, manual or breaking — and what "fixed" looks like in a number you can check.

02

The metric is the deliverable.

Latency, throughput, hours saved, efficiency gained. If a change can't be measured, it's decoration, and I'll say so before building it.

03

Range is the point.

Voice AI, data pipelines, factory-floor automation, frontend performance. Different stacks, same job every time: find the bottleneck, automate the tedious part, ship something reliable.

04

You'll always know where it stands.

Remote across US and Europe timezones since 2021. Clear updates, no surprises, and enough context to course-correct early instead of late.
